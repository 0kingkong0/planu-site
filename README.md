🌐 **Live Site:** [planu.ca](https://planu.ca)

---

## What is PlanU?

PlanU is an AI-powered academic planning tool built for students. Upload your syllabus and get your entire semester organized in under 60 seconds — deadlines, study sessions, and all.

This repository contains the **public landing page** for PlanU, hosted at [planu.ca](https://planu.ca).

---

## Features

- ⚡ Semester organized in under 60 seconds
- 📄 Syllabus upload and AI parsing
- 🗓️ Auto-generated study and deadline schedule
- 🎨 Clean, minimal UI with a signature receipt aesthetic
- 📱 Fully responsive design
